I am a Visiting Scholar at the [Institute of Political Science, Academia Sinica](https://www.ipsas.sinica.edu.tw/en/) and an Honorary Lecturer in the [Department of Government](https://www.essex.ac.uk/departments/government) at the [University of Essex](https://www.essex.ac.uk/). From 2023 to 2026, I was an Assistant Professor (Lecturer) in the Department of Government at Essex. Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the [Institute for Analytics and Data Science](https://www.essex.ac.uk/centres-and-institutes/institute-for-analytics-and-data-science), a Research Fellow in Data Science in the [Department of Methodology](https://www.lse.ac.uk/methodology) at the [London School of Economics and Political Science](https://www.lse.ac.uk/), and a Postdoctoral Research Officer at Nuffield College, University of Oxford. I received my Ph.D. in Political Science from [Rice University](https://politicalscience.rice.edu/) in 2012.

My research interests lie in data science and politics. In particular, I am interested in the statistical methodology for scaling political behavior and natural language processing of political texts, such as social media texts, government documents, and parliamentary speeches.
